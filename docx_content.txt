1. Mission
Pillar A converts raw official legal sources into a strict, inspectable, temporally ready database of legal article versions. It is responsible for source custody, OCR quality, structural parsing, bilingual alignment, schema migrations, validators, and fixtures used by the other pillars.
The first-month target is not a generic document parser. It is a legal-corpus ingestion pipeline that knows Tunisian legal hierarchy, article numbering patterns, Arabic/French variants, amendment-friendly identifiers, and traceability back to official source pages.

3. Raw Zone Contract
Raw files are immutable. Any correction, OCR output, parsed tree, or loaded database record must be a derived artifact with a run id. A reviewer must be able to trace any article version back to source file, page, region/span, OCR run, parser run, and loader run.

4. OCR and Layout Pipeline
Convert every PDF page to 300 dpi images with stable page ids.
Detect layout blocks: columns, headers, footers, article bodies, tables, marginal notes, page numbers, and footnote-like text.
Run Arabic and French OCR using language-specific settings; preserve page and block coordinates.
Apply constrained post-correction: the model may correct character-level OCR errors, but may not paraphrase, add, remove, summarize, or normalize legal meaning.
Compute CER against a manually transcribed 50-page reference set covering modern pages, older scans, bilingual pages, and pages with tables.
Send pages above thresholds to a review queue and exclude them from accepted ingestion until corrected.

5. Structural Parser
The parser is hybrid: deterministic rules handle standard legal patterns, while a schema-constrained LLM handles exceptional or ambiguous structures. The LLM is never trusted alone; every output passes validators before database load.
Parser Capability
Required Detail
Arabic article patterns
Recognize Arabic forms equivalent to Article 12, Article 12 repeated/bis, Article 12 third/ter, and embedded paragraph numbering.
French article patterns
Recognize Article 12, Article 12 bis, Article 12 ter, and related structural headings.
Hierarchy capture
Store book, title, chapter, section, subsection, article, paragraph, and table context as hierarchy_path.
Article id stability
Generate article_id from code_id plus normalized article number and hierarchy, not from version text.
Bilingual alignment
Align Arabic/French by official article number. If no official counterpart exists, mark it explicitly rather than silently translating.
Validation reports
Reject duplicate unresolved numbering, missing hierarchy, empty text, invalid dates, or broken source references.

6. Database and Loader Requirements
The loader must be idempotent by run id and source id. Re-running the same accepted input must not create duplicate article versions. Migrations must be versioned with Alembic and tested against a clean database.
Object
Requirement
legal_article_version
Must include version_id, article_id, code_id, article_number, hierarchy_path, text_ar, text_fr, valid_from, valid_to, status, source_ref, created_by_op.
Exclusion constraint
Prevent overlapping valid_from/valid_to windows for the same article_id.
Indexes
At minimum: code_id + article_number + valid_from, article_id + valid_from, and source_ref fields needed for audit.
Read views
Expose current_article_versions and article_versions_by_date for Pillar C.
Fixtures
Deliver 200 structured articles from all three month-one corpora as JSON plus a loading script by the end of week 1.
